# SMC-Crew Graveyard — Specific Failed Configurations

> **Critical design principle**: This graveyard records specific *configurations* that failed, NOT entire directions.
> A direction may be tested again with a different angle.
> Only close a direction entirely after 3+ distinct configurations fail with the same root cause.
>
> Each entry MUST include "What this does NOT rule out" and "Breakthrough angle" to prevent
> the solution space from being prematurely narrowed.

---

## Format Reference

```markdown
## G-<id>: <specific configuration title>
- Hypothesis ID: H-<N>
- Test iteration: #<N>
- Tested period: <date range>
- Metrics: WR=X%, RR=Y, weekly profit=$Z
- What specifically failed: [exact parameters that were tested]
- Why it failed: [root cause — market structure reason, not just "metrics were bad"]
- What this does NOT rule out: [adjacent angles in the same direction that remain open]
- Breakthrough angle: [if the core idea may still have merit, what different approach is worth trying]
```
